Getting Started

ProgressReveal helps you create professional before-and-after comparisons using photos from your iPhone.

Create Your First Project

  1. Open ProgressReveal.
  2. Go to the Capture or Library tab.
  3. Tap the + button to create a project.
  4. Enter a descriptive project name.
  5. Open the project.
  6. Add your BEFORE photo.
  7. Add your AFTER photo.
  8. Use the interactive comparison slider.
Tip: For the best results, photograph the same subject from approximately the same position and angle.

Interactive Before & After Comparisons

Once both photos are added, ProgressReveal creates an interactive comparison.

Horizontal Comparison

Drag the comparison divider left or right to reveal the before and after images.

Vertical Comparison

Switch the comparison orientation to Vertical. Drag the divider up or down to reveal changes.

Change Comparison Orientation

  1. Open a project with two photos.
  2. Find the Live Comparison section.
  3. Tap the Horizontal or Vertical control.
  4. Select your preferred comparison orientation.

You can drag directly across the comparison image or use the slider control beneath it.

Automatic Photo Alignment

ProgressReveal includes automatic photo alignment to help match before and after images.

Automatic Alignment

  1. Add your BEFORE and AFTER photos.
  2. Allow the app to process the images.
  3. Review the aligned comparison.
  4. Drag the slider to examine the results.

Re-align Photos

If the images do not match correctly, tap Re-align to try automatic alignment again.

Manually Adjust Alignment

  1. Open the Live Comparison section.
  2. Tap Adjust.
  3. Fine-tune the image alignment.
  4. Review the comparison.

Use Undo to reverse an applicable alignment adjustment.

Alignment Tip: Images with similar perspectives, recognizable details, and consistent framing typically align best. Large viewpoint changes or differences in zoom may reduce alignment accuracy.

Managing Projects

View Saved Projects

  1. Open the Library tab.
  2. Find the project you want to view.
  3. Tap the project to open it.

Rename a Project

  1. Open the project.
  2. Tap the project title at the top.
  3. Enter the new project name.
  4. Confirm the change.

Add Project Notes

  1. Open a project.
  2. Scroll to the Notes section.
  3. Enter your notes.
  4. Tap Save Project Details.

Delete a Project

  1. Open the project you want to remove.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the project.
  3. Tap the red Delete Project button.
  4. Review the confirmation dialog.
  5. Choose Confirm to delete or Cancel to keep it.
Important: Deleted project data may not be recoverable. Make sure you no longer need the project before confirming.

Custom Watermarks & Branding

Add your business branding or personal watermark to your comparison exports.

Add a Watermark

  1. Open a project.
  2. Scroll to View & Share.
  3. Tap Watermark.
  4. Enable Include Watermark.
  5. Enter watermark text or choose a logo.
  6. Adjust the watermark position and opacity.
  7. Tap Save.

Upload Your Logo

  1. Open the Project Watermark screen.
  2. Tap Add Logo or Change Logo.
  3. Select your logo image.
  4. Review the preview.
  5. Tap Save.

Adjust Watermark Appearance

Use the Position setting to place your watermark and the Opacity slider to control its transparency.

Remove a Logo

Open Project Watermark and tap Remove Logo. Save your changes.

Default Watermarks

Enable Make default for future projects to reuse your watermark preferences when creating new projects.

Enabled project watermarks can also appear in the live comparison preview.

Sharing Your Transformations

ProgressReveal offers several ways to share completed comparisons.

Share Current Image (JPG)

  1. Open a completed project.
  2. Adjust the slider to your preferred position.
  3. Scroll to View & Share.
  4. Tap Share Current Image (JPG).
  5. Choose where to save or share the image.

Share Interactive Slider (HTML)

  1. Open a project containing both photos.
  2. Go to View & Share.
  3. Tap Share Interactive Slider (HTML).
  4. Save or share the generated HTML file.

The HTML file contains the before and after images and allows recipients to interact with the comparison slider in a compatible web browser.

Note: Some messaging apps may require recipients to save the HTML file to Files before opening it in a browser. The recipient does not need ProgressReveal installed to view the HTML comparison.

Share Reveal Video (MP4)

  1. Open a completed comparison.
  2. Scroll to View & Share.
  3. Find Share Your Comparison.
  4. Tap Share Reveal Video.
  5. Wait for the video to finish processing.
  6. Save or share the resulting MP4 file.

Reveal videos begin with the BEFORE image and progressively reveal the AFTER image.

Sharing With a Watermark

Open the Watermark settings before sharing and confirm that Include Watermark is enabled if you want your branding on supported exports.

In-App Purchases

ProgressReveal may offer additional functionality through in-app purchases. Available offers and prices are displayed within the app before purchase.

Complete a Purchase

  1. Open the purchase screen in ProgressReveal.
  2. Review the available offer and price.
  3. Follow Apple's purchase confirmation process.
  4. Return to the app after confirmation.

Purchase Not Appearing?

  • Check that the purchase completed successfully.
  • Confirm you are using the appropriate Apple Account.
  • Close and reopen ProgressReveal.
  • Use Restore Purchases if that option is available.
  • Contact support if access is still missing.

Billing and Refunds

In-app purchases are processed through Apple. For billing questions, purchase history, or refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com .

Troubleshooting

Photos Are Not Aligning Correctly

  • Try the Re-align option.
  • Use Adjust to fine-tune alignment.
  • Confirm both images show the same subject.
  • Avoid extreme differences in angle or zoom.

Photos Will Not Load

  • Verify the photos are available on your iPhone.
  • Confirm any requested photo access permissions.
  • Try selecting the images again.
  • Restart ProgressReveal if necessary.

Reveal Video Will Not Export

  • Confirm the project includes both photos.
  • Make sure your iPhone has sufficient free storage.
  • Keep the app open during video processing.
  • Try exporting the video again.

Watermark Is Missing

  • Open the project's Watermark settings.
  • Ensure Include Watermark is enabled.
  • Check the selected logo and text.
  • Review the position and opacity.
  • Save your settings before exporting again.

The App Is Not Responding

  • Close and reopen ProgressReveal.
  • Check for an available app update.
  • Restart your iPhone.
  • Contact support if the issue continues.
Important: Do not uninstall the app as an initial troubleshooting step. Removing an app can also remove locally stored project data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create multiple projects?

Yes. Use the Library to organize and access multiple before-and-after projects.

Can I change the comparison direction?

Yes. ProgressReveal supports horizontal and vertical comparison sliders.

Does ProgressReveal align photos automatically?

Yes. Automatic alignment is available, and you can also re-align or manually adjust the result.

Can I add a business logo?

Yes. Add a logo or watermark text through the Project Watermark screen.

Can I change watermark opacity?

Yes. Use the Opacity slider in the watermark appearance settings.

Can I export a before-and-after video?

Yes. Use Share Reveal Video to generate an MP4 that progressively reveals the AFTER image.

Can other people interact with my comparison?

Yes. Share the HTML comparison. Recipients can open the file in a compatible browser and move the slider.

Does someone need the app to view my exported HTML?

No. The HTML comparison can be opened in a compatible web browser without installing ProgressReveal.

Can I delete projects?

Yes. Use Delete Project at the bottom of a project and confirm the deletion.

Can I switch between light and dark mode?

Yes. Open Settings to choose Light, Dark, or System appearance.
