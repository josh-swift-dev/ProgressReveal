Welcome to ProgressReveal Support

ProgressReveal makes it easy to capture, compare, align, organize, and share before-and-after photographs using your iPhone.

Getting Started

1. Create a Comparison

Open the Capture tab and tap "New Comparison."

2. Add a Before Photo

Capture a new photograph using your iPhone camera or import an existing image from your photo library.

3. Add an After Photo

Take or import the second photograph. ProgressReveal automatically attempts to align it with the original image.

4. Compare Your Photos

Drag the comparison slider to reveal the changes between your before-and-after photographs.

5. Share Your Progress

Export a standard JPG comparison or an interactive HTML slider that recipients can explore.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ProgressReveal require internet access?

No. Core features including photo capture, importing, alignment, comparison, and local storage work offline.

Sharing files through internet-based services may require a connection.

Can I import existing photographs?

Yes. You can import existing before-and-after images through Apple's photo picker.

How does automatic alignment work?

ProgressReveal uses Apple's on-device Vision technology to estimate positioning differences between photographs and attempt to align them.

For the best results, take both photographs from approximately the same position and angle.

What if my photographs don't align correctly?

Use Adjust to fine-tune positioning manually, or Re-align to retry automatic alignment.

Significant differences in perspective or camera angle may require retaking the photograph.

How do I share an interactive comparison?

Open a completed project and tap "Share Interactive Slider."

ProgressReveal creates an HTML file containing both photographs and an interactive comparison slider.

The recipient can open the file in a compatible browser without installing ProgressReveal. Some applications may require saving the file before opening it.

How do I share a regular picture?

Tap "Share Comparison Image" to export a standard JPG image showing the current slider position.

Where are my photographs stored?

Your comparison projects and photographs are saved locally on your iPhone.

ProgressReveal does not automatically upload them to an external server.

How do I delete a comparison?

Open the Library tab, press and hold a comparison, and select "Delete Comparison."

How do I change camera permissions?

Open iPhone Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Camera, and review ProgressReveal's access.

Can I use ProgressReveal for business?

Yes. ProgressReveal can help document landscaping, cleaning, restoration, remodeling, detailing, and other projects.

Troubleshooting

The camera won't open

Verify camera permissions in iPhone Settings and make sure your device camera is working properly.

Automatic alignment failed

Try using two photographs with similar framing, adequate lighting, and visible matching details. You can also adjust alignment manually.

My interactive file won't open

Some messaging applications do not directly preview HTML attachments. Try saving the file and opening it using a compatible browser.
