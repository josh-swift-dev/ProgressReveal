Welcome to ProgressReveal Support

ProgressReveal makes it easy to capture, compare, align, organize, and share before-and-after photographs using your iPhone.

Getting Started

1. Create a Comparison

Open the Capture tab and select "New Comparison."

2. Add Your Before Photo

Take a photograph using your iPhone camera or import an existing picture from your photo library.

3. Add Your After Photo

Capture or import your second photograph.

ProgressReveal automatically attempts to align the new picture with the original.

4. Compare Your Photos

Drag the interactive slider to reveal the differences between the two photographs.

5. Share Your Progress

Export a regular JPG comparison or an interactive HTML file containing both photographs and a working slider.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ProgressReveal require internet access?

No. Core features including photo capture, importing, alignment, comparison, and local storage work offline.

Sharing through certain services may require an internet connection.

Can I import existing photographs?

Yes. ProgressReveal allows you to import existing before-and-after photographs through Apple's system photo picker.

How does automatic alignment work?

ProgressReveal uses Apple's on-device Vision technology to estimate positioning differences between photographs.

For best results, capture both photographs from approximately the same position and angle.

What if automatic alignment isn't accurate?

Use Adjust to fine-tune the photograph's positioning manually.

You can also select Re-align to retry automatic alignment.

Significant differences in camera angle or perspective may require retaking the photograph.

How do I share an interactive comparison?

Open a completed comparison and select "Share Interactive Slider."

ProgressReveal generates an HTML file containing both photographs and an interactive comparison slider.

Recipients can open the file in a compatible browser without installing ProgressReveal. Some services require saving the file first.

How do I share a standard picture?

Select "Share Comparison Image" to export a JPG image showing the current slider position.

Where are my photographs stored?

Your comparison photographs and project information are stored locally on your device.

ProgressReveal does not automatically upload them to a developer-operated server.

How do I delete a comparison?

Open the Library tab, press and hold a comparison, and select "Delete Comparison."

Can I use ProgressReveal for business?

Yes. ProgressReveal can help document landscaping, cleaning, home renovation, vehicle detailing, restoration, and other projects.

Troubleshooting

The camera won't open

Open iPhone Settings, navigate to Privacy & Security, then Camera, and verify ProgressReveal has permission.

Automatic alignment failed

Try photographs with similar framing, adequate lighting, and visible matching details.

You can also adjust the alignment manually.

The interactive comparison won't open

Some messaging applications do not directly preview HTML attachments.

Try saving the file and opening it in a compatible web browser.
