Privacy Policy

Privacy Terms Support

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

ProgressReveal is an iOS application developed by Joshua Marcum that allows users to capture, import, align, compare, organize, and share before-and-after photographs.

1. Information Collection

ProgressReveal does not require an account and does not operate a developer-controlled service for collecting photographs or comparison projects.

The current version does not intentionally collect personal information, advertising identifiers, device location, or usage analytics through its core functionality.

2. Camera Access

ProgressReveal uses your iPhone camera only when you choose to capture a photograph.

Camera permission is managed by iOS and can be reviewed or changed through your device settings.

3. Photo Library Access

ProgressReveal uses Apple's system photo picker to allow you to select existing pictures.

Only photographs you explicitly choose are provided to the application through this picker. The app does not need unrestricted photo library access for importing pictures.

4. Local Photo Storage

Original photographs, aligned images, saved comparisons, project names, and notes are stored locally within the application's storage on your device.

These files are not automatically uploaded to a ProgressReveal server.

Device backup services may retain copies according to your Apple backup settings.

5. Automatic Alignment

ProgressReveal uses Apple's on-device image processing technologies, including the Vision framework, to attempt to align before-and-after photographs.

This functionality does not require your photographs to be transmitted to an external AI processing service.

6. Sharing and Exporting

ProgressReveal allows you to export standard comparison images and interactive HTML files.

Interactive HTML exports contain embedded copies of your before-and-after photographs so recipients can use the comparison slider.

Sharing occurs when you initiate it. Once a file is shared, the receiving application or recipient may store, process, or redistribute it according to their own practices.

7. Advertising and Tracking

The current version of ProgressReveal does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking functionality.

8. Retention and Deletion

Saved comparisons remain within the application until deleted by the user or removed with the application.

Deleting a comparison removes its associated locally stored photographs and alignment files. Previously exported or shared copies must be deleted separately.

Information retained in device backups may be subject to Apple's backup retention practices.

9. Children's Privacy

ProgressReveal does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-controlled online service.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.
