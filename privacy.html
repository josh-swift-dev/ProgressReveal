Privacy Policy

Privacy Terms Support

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

ProgressReveal is an iOS application that allows users to capture, import, align, compare, organize, and share before-and-after photographs.

Your privacy is important to us. ProgressReveal is designed to process photographs locally on your device without requiring an account or cloud-based photo processing.

1. Information Collection

ProgressReveal does not require account registration and does not operate a developer-controlled service for collecting your photographs or comparison projects.

The current version does not intentionally collect personal information, device location, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics through its core features.

2. Camera Access

ProgressReveal accesses your iPhone camera only when you choose to take a photograph.

Camera permission is controlled by iOS and can be managed through your device's privacy settings.

3. Photo Library Access

ProgressReveal uses Apple's system photo picker to import existing photographs.

The application receives only the photographs you explicitly select. Unrestricted photo library access is not required for importing images.

4. Local Storage

Original photographs, aligned photographs, comparison projects, project titles, and notes are stored locally within the application's storage on your device.

ProgressReveal does not automatically upload these files to an external server.

Device backup services may retain copies according to your Apple backup settings.

5. Automatic Photo Alignment

ProgressReveal uses Apple's on-device image processing technologies, including the Vision framework, to attempt to align before-and-after photographs.

Photo alignment is performed locally and does not require transmitting photographs to an external AI processing service.

6. Sharing and Exporting

ProgressReveal allows users to export standard comparison images and interactive HTML comparison files.

Interactive HTML exports contain embedded copies of both photographs, allowing recipients to operate the comparison slider.

Sharing occurs only when initiated by the user. Receiving applications and services may process or retain shared files according to their own privacy policies.

7. Advertising and Tracking

The current version of ProgressReveal does not include third-party advertising, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking functionality.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Comparison projects remain stored locally until deleted by the user or removed with the application.

Deleting a comparison removes its associated locally stored images and alignment data.

Previously shared or exported files must be deleted separately from their destinations.

9. Children's Privacy

ProgressReveal does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated online service.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or privacy practices.

Updates will be published on this page.
